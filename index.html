PRODUCT 01 Working — try it in the demo
Kinetic CAPTCHA
KC-1 Platform + Presence

At login, the verification target moves to a random screen position. How long you must hold is chosen by the server and never sent to the page: the page only learns a hold is over when the server says so. A script can’t read the answer in advance, so bots stand out while people pass.

+ PRESENCE LAYER — After login, a silent behavioral agent monitors mouse entropy, typing cadence, and scroll patterns to build a live human-presence score. When the score drops — session hijacking, bot handoff, credential takeover — Anya triggers a kinetic re-challenge automatically. Zero interruption for real humans. Automated takeover fails instantly.

Cloud Deflection Shield
ICDS Platform

Attack traffic is automatically routed to a sacrificial cloud layer that absorbs and resets — your physical server never sees a single malicious packet. Visitors who passed the Kinetic check keep their place in line; known repeat attackers are refused on arrival.

File Authorization Protocol
RFAP Platform

Every download, print, or share on your encrypted files triggers a real-time push to you. You approve or deny — live, every time. Stolen credentials still cannot open your files without your active permission. Unlike standing permissions, each approval works once and expires in 60 seconds.

PATENT CLAIMS — TECHNICAL SUMMARY
What makes each invention novel
01
KC-1 Novel Claim
The server randomizes both where the target appears and how long it must be held. The hold duration is never transmitted to the client; the server alone signals when it has elapsed, then checks the recorded movement against its own copy.
02
ICDS Novel Claim
Intentional, autonomous destruction of cloud infrastructure as primary defense mechanism. Prior art (Cloudflare, Akamai) uses rate limiting and traffic filtering — not disposable architecture.
03
RFAP Novel Claim
Real-time owner push notification with approve/deny gating before decryption token issuance. Prior art (IRM, DRM systems) grants standing permissions — not per-event live owner approval.
HOW IT COMPARES
What’s already on the market, and what we do differently
Kinetic CAPTCHA
Closest today

Puzzle and drag CAPTCHAs (GeeTest, Arkose Labs); invisible scoring (reCAPTCHA v3, hCaptcha, Turnstile)

What’s different

They score behaviour too. The difference: our server picks where the target is and how long to hold, never tells the page the hold time, then keeps checking presence after you pass.

Cloud Deflection Shield
Closest today

Edge networks (Cloudflare, Akamai, AWS Shield); moving-target-defence research

What’s different

They absorb far bigger floods than we can today. The difference: people who passed the Kinetic check keep their place in line while unverified traffic is turned away first.

File Authorization
Closest today

Rights management (Microsoft Purview, Seclore, Fasoo); push approval for logins (Duo, Okta)

What’s different

They grant standing permission you can revoke later. The difference: every open asks the owner live, and the key works once for 60 seconds.

We name these so you can check for yourself. Each of the three on its own has neighbours; connecting them through one human check is the part we haven’t found elsewhere.
