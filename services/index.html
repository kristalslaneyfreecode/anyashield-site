Services

Website security for small firms.

I review and rebuild websites for small professional practices around Austin — the ones holding client money, client records, or both. Fixed prices, written in plain English, no jargon and no retainer.

Built for: law firms, accountants and bookkeepers, title companies, insurance agencies, medical and dental practices, property managers — anywhere from one person to about twenty, with no IT department.

What I check

All of it from outside, the way any visitor sees your site. Nothing is probed, scanned or accessed.

  • Encryption. Whether your site is secure, and whether it stays that way.
  • Email spoofing. Whether a stranger can send email that looks like it came from you.
  • Your contact form. Whether messages actually arrive. They often don't.
  • Exposure. Admin pages, file listings and leftovers visible from outside.
  • Software age. What your site runs on and how far behind it is.
  • Phones. Whether half your visitors can read it.
  • Speed. How long people wait before anything appears.
  • Privacy. Whether you collect personal details without a policy.

How it works

  1. You send me your website address. That's all I need to start. No access, no passwords, nothing installed.
  2. I check it and write it up. Two pages within three business days: what I found, how serious each thing is, and what to do about it in order.
  3. You decide what happens next. Fix it yourself, hand the report to whoever manages your site, or have me do the work. Plenty of people take the report and stop there, and that's a perfectly good outcome.
